Test engineer with a physicist's training.

I design and automate tests for systems that combine hardware, firmware and software, and write the verification documentation that goes with them. Based in Motala, Sweden.

Outside work I have a wife and two children, I like to cook, and I spent years coaching freerunning and gymnastics.

How I work

  • Requirements before tests

    Requirements and test specifications in requirement-management tools, every test traced to a requirement, then run hands-on in the lab across hardware, firmware and software.

  • Automate what repeats

    Automated production and system tests, from instrument control and signal routing to the finished report, and scripts and simulators that turn manual lab steps into repeatable runs.

  • Data before conclusions

    Six years of research projects built on simulations, statistics and data analysis, from Monte Carlo models to supercomputer runs, always checked against measurements.

  • Documentation that gets used

    Formal verification documents, test reports and specifications used across teams, and scientific papers and conference talks.
